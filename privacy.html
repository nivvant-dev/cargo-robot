1. Who we are

This website presents a student-built prototype created for the NASA HUNCH program. The project team is based at Ranchview High School. For this policy, the team and school mentor are responsible for how information sent to the contact address is handled.

This is an independent educational project. It is not an official NASA website and is not operated, reviewed or endorsed by NASA. See the Terms of Use for the full disclaimer.

2. What this site collects — and what it does not

The site itself does not ask you to create an account or submit personal information. In particular, it does not provide:

  • accounts, passwords or sign-in;
  • a contact form, upload form or newsletter;
  • payment processing or donations;
  • analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or social media plugins;
  • embedded videos, maps or other third-party content.

The trial log is the only information the site creates. It is stored by your browser, not on a server operated by the team. No email address, name or other personal detail is required to use the site.

Please do not enter sensitive personal information into the optional notes field. Those notes stay on your device, but anyone with access to your browser profile may be able to read them.

3. Data stored in your browser

When you add a trial in the Test Results section, the page saves the payload, result, cycle time, optional notes and a random record ID in your browser's localStorage under the key icur-trials-v1.

Trial data is not sent to the team, NASA, the school or any other party by this site. It is specific to the browser and device you use. If you use a different browser or device, the log will be empty there.

You can remove the log at any time with Clear all trials in the Test Results section, or by clearing site data / local storage in your browser settings. Removing site data does not affect the rest of the website.

4. Hosting and technical logs

This is a static site. It may be hosted on GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages or another static host. Like most web hosts, these providers may record standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages or files requested, and the time of the request. Those logs are used to deliver the site, diagnose problems and protect the service.

The team does not use hosting logs to identify visitors, build profiles or market to anyone. The hosting provider processes this information under its own privacy policy. You can read the policies of common static hosts at GitHub and Netlify.

If the site is opened directly from a local file, no hosting provider is involved at all; the page runs entirely on your computer.

5. Email contact

The contact links on this site open your own email application. If you choose to email the team, your email address and message will be received by the team's school or mentor mailbox. We use them only to understand and reply to your message, and we do not add you to a mailing list or sell your details.

Email can be retained for as long as needed to handle the conversation, or as required by school record-keeping rules. Please do not send passwords, identity documents, health information or other sensitive data. If your message is about accessibility, see the Accessibility Statement for what to include.

6. Cookies and analytics

This site does not set cookies and does not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, social tracking or any similar technology. That is why there is no cookie banner: there are no optional cookies to consent to.

The trial log uses localStorage, which works similarly to a cookie in that it is stored by your browser, but it is not sent with web requests and cannot be read by another website. It is used only for the function described above. The site also respects your browser's reduced-motion preference and does not use it for tracking.

7. Students and minors

This site is an educational project, likely to be viewed by students, teachers, mentors and reviewers. It is not directed at collecting information from children. We do not ask for names, ages, school records or contact details through the site.

If you are a minor and want to contact the team, please use a school or mentor email address and ask a teacher, mentor or guardian to help with the message. The local trial log can be cleared at any time and is never transmitted to us.

8. Your choices and rights

Because the site does not create a server-side profile of you, there is no account to close and no online record for the team to retrieve. You can:

  • delete your trial log at any time using Clear all trials;
  • clear all site data through your browser's privacy settings;
  • choose not to email us, or ask us to delete an email conversation;
  • contact us with a privacy question or concern.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. We will respond to reasonable requests about information we actually hold (for example, an email you sent) and will explain if we cannot fulfil a request and why.

9. Security and external links

The site is served over HTTPS by its host, uses a Content Security Policy, avoids third-party scripts and does not store secrets in its code. No website can promise perfect security; see the Security Policy for what is in place and how to report a problem.

This site links to external websites such as hosting providers' privacy policies. Those sites are not controlled by the team and have their own privacy practices. A link is not an endorsement of everything on the destination site.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the project or the website changes. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will describe it on this page. Continued use of the site after an update means you are using the site under the revised policy.
